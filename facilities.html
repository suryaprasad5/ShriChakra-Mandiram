Temple Facilities

Spaces created for prayer, yagna, prasada, learning, seva, and future Shreevidya activities.

Temple Infrastructure

Sacred Facilities

VIDYARANYA PRARTHANA MANDIRA

The temple is accompanied by an adequate Prarthana Mandira called Vidyaranya Prarthana Mandira to accommodate about 100 people for Dhyana, Sankeerthana, Bhajan, and similar devotional activities.

SHREEVIDYA CHIDYAGNA SHALA

It is believed that the chaithanya in the deity can be established by doing Yagna; and hence, a unique yaga shala called Shreevidya Yagna Shala is also constructed and yagnic activities are carried here by the devotees.

ANNAPOORNAMBA PRASADA BHOJANA SHALA

This is constructed to provide prasadam to devotees to an extent of 50 members at a time.

SHREE BHARATI GOSHAALA

A sacred facility dedicated to goshala seva and the traditional care of cows within the spiritual ecosystem of the temple.
