Personal Services

Sevas for Devotees

📿

Nama Archana

Personal Archana with your name and nakshatra offered to the Devi using 108 or 1008 names from Lalita Sahasranama. Includes prasadam.

Starting from ₹108
💧

Abhishekam

Sacred bathing of the deity with panchamrita (milk, curd, honey, ghee, sugarcane juice), then with fresh water and flowers. Highly auspicious.

Starting from ₹501
🔥

Ganapati Homa

Fire ritual to Lord Ganesha to remove obstacles before beginning new ventures, journeys, studies, or life events. Modaka naivedya included.

Starting from ₹1,100
🌺

Chandi Homa

Powerful fire ritual based on Devi Mahatmyam (700 verses). Conducted for protection, wellbeing, and victory over adversity. Duration: 4–6 hours.

Starting from ₹5,100
⭐

Navagraha Puja

Propitiation of all nine planetary deities. Ideal for new beginnings, resolving astrological challenges, or during Sade Sati and Dasha periods.

Starting from ₹1,501
🕯️

Sahasra Deepa Puja

Lighting of 1,008 lamps before the Devi — one of the most auspicious sevas. Especially offered on Fridays and Navaratri evenings.

Starting from ₹2,100
💍

Marriage Blessing Puja

Special puja at the temple for couples seeking Devi's blessings for a harmonious and prosperous married life. Includes Kanya Puja and Sumangali Prarthana.

Starting from ₹3,100
👶

Namakarana & Annaprasana

Sacred naming ceremony and first solid food ritual for infants, conducted with full Vedic procedures at the temple or at home by our priests.

Starting from ₹2,500
🏠

Grihapravesh Puja

Vastu Puja and housewarming ritual to consecrate a new home and invite divine blessings into it. Includes Punyahavachanam and Navagraha puja.

Starting from ₹4,500
