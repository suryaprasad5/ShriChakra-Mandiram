Our Purpose

Why ShriChakra Mandiram Exists

The ShriChakra — the sacred geometric diagram of the Divine Mother — is considered the most powerful of all yantras in the Shakta tradition. Our temple was founded with a singular purpose: to make this living science accessible to sincere devotees, and to maintain an unbroken tradition of its worship.

We are not merely a place of religious ritual, but a living university of spiritual wisdom, where the Vedic, Tantric, and Puranic streams converge in service of the Divine Mother.

Every stone, every ritual, every mantra here is calibrated to the sacred geometry of the ShriChakra, making this entire space a three-dimensional yantra.

Temple History

Five Decades of Devotion

1973

Founding & Consecration

Sri Pandit Ramachandra Shastri, a renowned Shakta scholar from Varanasi, consecrated the original ShriChakra yantra on Navaratri Mahanavami, establishing the first puja in a humble shed. The presiding deity, Devi Rajeshwari, was installed following Tantric tradition.

1985

Main Temple Structure Built

With community support, the current granite temple was constructed following Agama Shastra guidelines. The gopuram was consecrated by Shankaracharya Sri Abhinava Vidyatirtha Mahaswami during his historic Bangalore visit.

1998

The Great Kumbhabhishekam

A 10-day Maha Kumbhabhishekam was conducted with over 108 priests from across South India. The Shodasha Mahabhishekam of the Devi and the renovation of the ShriChakra yantra renewed the divine presence of the temple.

2010

Cultural & Educational Wing

The Shakti Vidyapeetha was established — offering courses in Vedic chanting, Sanskrit, Indian classical music, and Yantra Shastra. Over 500 students have graduated from these programs.

2023

Golden Jubilee Celebration

The 50th anniversary was celebrated with a grand 21-day Ati Rudra Maha Yagna, Sahasra Deepotsava, and the installation of a newly carved Panchadhatu ShriChakra Meru — gifted by devotees from 12 countries.
