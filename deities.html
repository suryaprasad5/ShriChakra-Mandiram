The Divine Pantheon

The presiding deities of ShriChakra Mandiram, each an aspect of the one Supreme Shakti — Devi Tripura Sundari.

🕉️
Shodasha Mahabhishekam · Daily

Devi Tripura Sundari

Lalita · Rajeshwari · Mahatripurasundari

Tripura Sundari — "She who is beautiful in all three worlds" — is the Supreme Goddess of the Sri Vidya tradition and the presiding deity of this temple. She is the sixteenth Nitya (eternal form), known as Shodashi, and is considered the most complete manifestation of the Divine Mother.

Her yantra, the ShriChakra, consists of nine interlocking triangles surrounding a central bindu, representing the union of Shiva (masculine principle) and Shakti (feminine principle) at 43 sacred points, each inhabited by a deity.

She holds a sugarcane bow (desire), flower arrows (the five senses), a noose (bondage she frees us from), and a goad (liberation). Her red complexion symbolizes the fire of consciousness that pervades all creation.

Also Worshipped

The Temple Pantheon

🔱

Lord Shiva – Chandramauleshwara

North Shrine · Pradosham Puja

As the masculine half of the Ardhanarishvara, Shiva is inseparable from Shakti. His form here as Chandramauleshwara — the crescent-moon crowned — symbolizes the cooling grace of consciousness.

🦚

Lord Murugan – Subramanya

East Shrine · Friday Puja

The warrior son of Shiva and Parvati, known as Skanda and Kartikeya. Worshipped here as protector and granter of wisdom, with a grand chariot festival conducted annually.

🐘

Lord Ganesha – Mahaganapati

Entrance Shrine · All Days

The elephant-headed remover of obstacles stands guard at the temple entrance. Every ritual begins and ends with Ganesha puja. Sankashti Chaturthi is celebrated monthly.

🌺

Devi Saraswati

Library Shrine · Saraswati Puja

Goddess of knowledge, arts, and wisdom. A special abhishekam is conducted every Vijayadashami when all books, instruments, and tools are blessed in the tradition of Ayudha Puja.

💛

Devi Mahalakshmi

South Shrine · Friday Puja

The Goddess of abundance, fortune, and prosperity. Lotus offerings and special turmeric abhishekam are conducted every Friday. A grand Varalakshmi Vratam is held annually.

🐒

Lord Hanuman

West Shrine · Tuesday & Saturday

The embodiment of devotion, strength, and service. Sundarakanda parayana is conducted every Tuesday evening, drawing devotees seeking courage and protection.

Planetary Deities

Navagraha Shrine

The nine planetary deities are installed in the traditional circular formation in the Navagraha Shrine. Special abhishekam for each graha is performed on their corresponding day.
