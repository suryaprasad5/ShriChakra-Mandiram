March – May 2026

Mar 6
Maha Shivaratri Special Puja
All Night · Special Event
▼

An all-night celebration beginning with special Rudrabishekam at 6 PM. The Shiva lingam will receive Panchamrita abhishekam every 3 hours through the night — at 9 PM, 12 AM, 3 AM, and 6 AM. Bilva archana, Shiva Sahasranama recitation, and Maha Aarti at dawn. Temple open all night. Free prasadam for all devotees.

📍 Main Temple · 🕕 Begins 6 PM · 🆓 Free Entry
Mar 21
Ugadi Mahotsavam
Telugu New Year · Festival
▼

Telugu and Kannada New Year celebration with pre-dawn Panchangam reading, Ugadi Pachadi offering, and special kalasha puja at 6 AM. Cultural programs including Carnatic music concert in the evening. Traditional feast (saptha bhojanam) served to 500+ devotees at noon.

📍 Main Temple & Hall · 🕕 6 AM – 9 PM · 🆓 Free Entry & Meals
Apr 6
Sri Ram Navami Mahotsav
3-Day Celebration · Mahotsav
▼

A 3-day event (Apr 4–6) celebrating the birth of Sri Rama. Day 1: Sundarakanda Parayana. Day 2: Ramayana Harikatha by renowned scholar. Day 3 (Ram Navami): Maha Abhishekam of Rama at noon, Kalyanam (celestial wedding) ceremony, and grand procession around the temple. Prasadam distribution throughout.

📍 Main Hall · 🕕 Apr 4–6, 6 AM onwards · 🆓 Free
Apr 13
Vishu & Tamil New Year
Seasonal Festival
▼

Pre-dawn Vishukkani darshan — auspicious viewing of the Devi adorned with gold, fruits, coins, and flowers — is arranged for devotees from 4 AM. Special Tamil Panchanga recitation and Thai Pongal-style naivedyam. Evening cultural program with Bharatanatyam performance dedicated to Devi.

📍 Main Temple · 🕓 4 AM – 9 PM · 🆓 Free Entry
Apr 14
Hanuman Jayanti
Festival · Community
▼

Birthday of Lord Hanuman celebrated with Sundarakanda Akanda Parayana (continuous recitation from dawn to dusk), 108 Hanuman Chalisa recitation by devotees, Vada mala and Panchakajjaya offerings, and evening Sundara Kanda Harikatha.

📍 Hanuman Shrine & Main Hall · 🕕 6 AM – 8 PM
Apr 30
Monthly Amavasya Puja
Monthly Tithi · Regular
▼

New Moon puja with special Pitru Tarpana, Kala Bhairava puja, and Devi Archana. Ideal for ancestral remembrance and seeking blessings for deceased family members. Annadanam (free meal) served after puja. Conducted on every Amavasya (new moon day).

📍 Main Temple · 🕕 8 AM – 1 PM · 🆓 Free

Annual Highlights

The Great Festivals

🌟

Navaratri Mahotsavam

Nine nights of intense worship of the nine forms of Devi. Special alankara (decoration) each night, Kumari Puja on Ashtami, and Saraswati Puja on Navami. Grand Vijayadashami procession.

October 2026
🪔

Karthigai Deepam

Festival of lights where 10,000 lamps illuminate the entire temple complex. Skanda Sashti, Soubhagya Lakshmi Puja, and grand Deepotsava create a breathtaking spectacle.

November 2026
🎆

Diwali Mahalakshmi Puja

Grand Lakshmi puja on Amavasya night with 1,008 lamps. Special Kuber Puja and business account blessing (Chopda Puja) performed on Pratipada.
